Chapter 1: A last stand

Danmachi fanfiction

The Homunculus without a name stood alone, but through the sacrifice of his friend, he now wished to live. 


I stood upon the battlefield, once a suburban neighbourhood, now laid to ruin. An acceptable loss. I was now facing the last Master in this war besides my creator. I have fought the other Masters and won, but now my purpose was at an end, for I was only a puppet made to grant him victory. 

Standing before me was my servant Rider. He had taken much damage throughout this war, yet he stands before me. His armour is scratched and missing pieces. His shield has dents and his sword is chipped. Wounds cover the parts his armour can’t cover. According to my calculations, he was at his limit. His steed, a massive grey warhorse, stands beside him, equally damaged. Yet, despite his wounds, he smiled. 

Across from us slowly dissipating into motes of golden dust was the second-to-last Servant. The Lancer of this war, Romulus, had been a dangerous foe. With the might of his lance and the cunning of his Master, he had been a constant thorn in our side through the war. 

“You have fought well Rider, truly a battle worthy of the glory of Rome. Had the interloper not intervened we would have had a glorious last stand.” Romulus bellowed to us.

“In another life then friend. I shall see you in the throne of heroes. We shall drink until dawn!” Rider cheered. 

The interloper was my creator, the Master of Assassin. Hundreds of skull-faced humans stood around us. The data in my head already identifies the Servant. Hassan of the Hundred Faces. Truly, a Servant who suits him, for he hides behind masks from everyone. 

The formation of Assassin split, and with a slow walk, I saw my creator. “Well done, puppet, truly I couldn’t have hunted these fools without you.” In his hand was the head of Lancer’s Master. Blood dripping upon the ground. 

Puppet, the only thing my creator ever called me. With my purpose completed, I approached him. As we had planned, he shall now kill me to get the wish. Before I could even take two steps, Rider's arm blocked my path.

“Giving up already? Through this entire war, you have shown me courage that would even impress my soldiers. Shouldn’t we finish this as is proper?”

“Why? My purpose is fulfilled, my death is now. My courage, as you put it, was merely calculations of our foes and acceptable losses”

“What about your wish?”

“There was never a wish for me.”

“Then I shall grant you a wish.” He handed me his sword and shield, not just to hold, but to merge with my being. 

“I shall show you the magnificent light that is being alive, and you shall witness what I have seen in you all along. Now stand back, for my charge is here”

“But what about your wish?” I couldn't help but ask, the words coming before I could realise that I had spoken them.

“This was my wish all along, a final charge against insurmountable numbers, a glorious stand to redeem myself and my knights who rode with me. Stand back master for I shall unleash my Noble Phantasm. The Phantasm of I Count Alaric of Westhaven!”

I couldn’t stop him. I had already used all command spells during the war. All I could do was watch him throw away his life for an object, but watching his back I felt a spark in my being. It was the first time I had ever understood the word beautiful. For that is what his resolve was.

“My sword is broken.
My shield is shattered.
My steed has fallen.”

He mounted his horse as a phalanx of spectral knights formed around their count.


“Behind me lies everything I swore to protect.
Before me lies the enemy.
Then there is only one road left.
Ride, Griefmane!”

He charged towards Assassin, his knights following, even knowing this was the end.

“Raise the banner!
Sound the charge!
This is my final battle!
This is my final oath!

Enveloped in a golden light that lit up the surroundings, all I could think was ‘Is this what it means to live?’

“THE ROAD ENDS HERE -
MY FINAL CHARGE”

The world exploded with light. I fell to my knees as I felt his Saint Graph break. 

When the light died down, there was no more Assassin and no more Rider. I cradled the sword and shield in my hands. A new feeling welled up inside me. I couldn’t understand it. It hurt my chest, but there was no blow.  All I could do was stare at the spot my Servant, no, my friend, had shown why he was a legend. 

Steps echoed out in the silence. My creator walked towards me with contempt in his eyes. “Really, you couldn’t just die. Honestly, you truly are a failure. If you didn’t have enough Mana Circuits to summon a Servant, I would have destroyed you like the rest.”

A heavy kick landed on my side, and I fell to the ground. The creator raised his hand, a dark orb formed in his palm. “I think it's time to get. MY. WISH!” His arm struck downward, but just before it connected, I stabbed him with the sword. It pierced his heart.

My eyes widened at what I had done. This was simply unthinkable. Yet I had struck down my creator. I watched the light leave his eyes, but his attack still connected. All I could think was, ‘I want to live.’

Unbeknownst to me, the Grail heard my wish. As the last Master standing, I had won, but the Grail couldn’t save me. Not in this world. So I was sent to another. Where I could truly learn to live. 



POV: Hel

I wandered down the road towards Orario, finally having been allowed to travel to Genkai. I couldn’t wait to scare my father. Taking in the scenery around me made me appreciate the mortal world all the more. The lush green trees and the gentle feeling of the wind made it all worth it. 

Suddenly, I felt something nearby. A soul that wasn’t there before. My curiosity got the better of me and I decided to investigate. Walking through the undergrowth of the forest, deflecting branches that would have hit me in the face, I finally saw what I had felt. 

It was a mortal child, but different from the ones I had seen in my realm. This one had long white hair, dull red eyes and was wearing dust-covered white clothes. The child also held a sword and shield in his arms, although they looked almost broken, as if having gone through a war. 

That, however, wasn’t the strangest thing about the child. His body was segmented like a doll. You could see the places where the pieces were assembled. The child was covered in injuries, but that wasn’t even the worst of it. His soul was so small, tiny even. I didn’t know how it had survived this long. I knew that if nothing was done, this brilliant, but tiny soul would end.

Making a decision in haste, I took the child with me and granted it my Falna. A familia even before reaching Orario. Truly, the heavens had strange plans.
